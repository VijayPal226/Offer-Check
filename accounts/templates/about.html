ABOUT OFFER CHECK

Helping you make informed decisions about opportunities.

Finding an internship, company or training program is easy. Understanding whether an opportunity is worth exploring can take much more research.

OfferCheck brings important opportunity-related information together so that students and job seekers can research before they apply.

💡

Why OfferCheck?

Because an opportunity deserves a little research before you spend your time, effort and expectations on it.

THE PROBLEM

Opportunity research can be confusing.

Students often find opportunities through different websites, social media, messages and online communities. Important details can be scattered across multiple places.

🔍
Too Much Searching

Relevant information may be spread across different sources, making research time-consuming.

🧩
Information Is Scattered

Company, internship and training details may not be available together in one organized place.

❓
More Verification May Be Needed

Not every piece of online information should be accepted without checking its source.

OUR APPROACH

Research first. Decide yourself.

OfferCheck is designed to organize information rather than make decisions for users.

Users can explore available information, understand what has been documented and identify areas where additional verification may be useful.

📋
