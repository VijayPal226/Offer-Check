Red Flags

Watch out for these warning signs
⚠️ They refuse to share CIN or registration details
⚠️ They only operate through WhatsApp/Telegram
⚠️ They ask for money upfront without showing legal documents
⚠️ They guarantee 100% job placement without proper process
⚠️ They don't provide detailed curriculum or trainer information
⚠️ Important: If you notice multiple red flags, it's best to avoid the training program. Legitimate providers are transparent about their operations and curriculum.
